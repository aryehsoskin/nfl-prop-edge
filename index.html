How to read this

Fair % is the market's true probability: every other book's price with its margin stripped out, averaged. Edge is what you'd make per $100 over the long run at Hard Rock's price. 0% = break-even; anything positive means Hard Rock is paying more than the bet is worth.

Market line is where the other books have the line. When it's in bold, the market has moved away from Hard Rock's line, and green bold means that gap favors your side. That gap is where Hard Rock value usually comes from. Edges above ~10% can mean injury news, so check before betting.
